## What is W1AW/6

PAARA has run the Pacificon Special Event Station for many years. For the last seven, Mikko ([AB6RF](https://www.qrz.com/db/AB6RF){:target="_blank"}) has managed it.

The setup resembles PAARA's ARRL Field Day operation:
* Each station has an HF rig, logging computer, headsets, CW paddle, and an antenna for its band.
* On 10m, 15m, and 20m we use the PAARA tower trailer and the KT-34A 4-element tri-band beam.
* On the lower bands we use wire antennas.
* The CW and SSB stations usually run a linear amplifier.

### Station lineup

The lineup is not settled yet, but expect five or six stations, most locked to a single band. As we did last time, we plan a dedicated FT8 station, and we will probably run WSPR as well.

We also expect to experiment with a mode new to us this year: [FreeDV](https://freedv.org/){:target="_blank"}, open-source digital voice for HF.

### ARRL Station Manager

The ARRL Station Manager makes the [W1AW/6](https://www.qrz.com/db/W1AW/6){:target="_blank"} call sign available to us. We apply well in advance and receive written authorization for those specific days. They also handle the QSL cards and LoTW confirmations once we send them a composite log of every contact made.

## Running the station

From Friday noon to Sunday noon, Pacificon visitors can make HF contacts on [W1AW/6](https://www.qrz.com/db/W1AW/6){:target="_blank"} under our supervision.

A control operator must be present the whole weekend. Mikko ([AB6RF](https://www.qrz.com/db/AB6RF){:target="_blank"}), Christopher ([AI6KG](https://www.qrz.com/db/AI6KG){:target="_blank"}), and other designees take that duty. The control operator on watch:
* Answers for the station's transmissions
* Guides the visiting operators
* Keeps an eye on the equipment

You need not be a control operator to help. Anyone may get on the air under the supervision of whoever has the watch.

PAARA owns a couple of HF rigs and the logging computers. Members lend the rest.
