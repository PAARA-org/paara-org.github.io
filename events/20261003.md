# 2026/10/03 PAARA In The Park

* **When**: October 3rd 2026
* **Where**: Memorial Park, Cupertino. Parking off of Alves Dr -- lot, street, or the Quinlan Community Center.
* **Time**: 10:00am - 5:00pm

## Everyone's invited

Please join us for yet another PAARA In The Park event, back in the Cupertino Memorial Park. Loads of fun, people of all ages to socialize with, lots of hams there to answer questions, etc.  Come and just sit and chat, operate our HF, or participate in the featured activity.

<mark>Free lunch and drinks provided by the club!</mark>

## Building a tape-measure Yagi-Uda

[`Justin Giorgi, AI6YM`](https://ai6ym.radio){:target="_blank"} has designed an easy-to-build version of the 2m Tape-Measure Yagi.

If you are interested in building an antenna, you must sign up online using [this form](https://forms.gle/mxSu5KiWEfv7Vq7e7){:target="_blank"}.

* **Build start time**: `10:30am`
* **Build end time**: `12:30pm`
* **Cost**: `$10/antenna`

<div class="custom-notice">
  From 10:30am until 3:00pm, there will also be a number of <b>Fox transmitters</b> in the field for direction finding practice with your newly built antenna.
</div>

## How do I get there?

We'll be in the Group Picnic Area ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XSFCQeAYGcV9ynZSA){:target="_blank"}) of [Cupertino Memorial Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LaJSeRZNvjQJM8Ft6).

## Parking 

Several parking options for Memorial Park are located along **Alves Dr.** (off of Stelling):

* Smallish lot for the park -- may be full, and is partially occupied by a construction area
* Street parking along Alves
* In the Community Center lot (Quinlan Community Center, which is closed on weekends).

(Navigation apps may take you to Stevens Creek Blvd for the park but that is not where the parking is.  Do not park in the the Sports Center lot.   The Senior Center lot is mainly permit parking only, with a few non-permit spots, usually full.)
