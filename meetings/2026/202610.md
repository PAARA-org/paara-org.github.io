## Details

### Topic #1: All-Year DX Contest

Kent Olsen, [N6WT](https://www.qrz.com/db/N6WT){:target="_blank"}, began his ham radio journey in 1998, when a fellow member of his off-road club, the **Desert Dawgs**, encouraged the group to earn their Technician licenses. Now retired after 26 years as a UPS feeder driver, Kent lives on 16 acres outside Arroyo Grande with his wife, Gail [KJ6CPH](https://www.qrz.com/db/KJ6CPH){:target="_blank"}.

He earned his General and Extra licenses in 2014, and today he chases DX (318 DXCC confirmed, DXCC Challenge 2265), enjoys contesting, and operates **Summits on the Air** - a great way to stay in shape and get out of the shack.

### Topic #2: Helioclock: A Living World Clock for the Ham Shack

Helioclock is a real-time situational awareness display built for amateur radio operators, showing live propagation data, space weather, DX spots, lightning, radar, satellite passes, and more, all on one always-on screen. In this talk, Anthony Watts [KA9NWM](https://www.qrz.com/db/KA9NWM){:target="_blank"} will cover what Helioclock does, the story behind building it as a spiritual successor to the beloved HamClock project, and a live demo of the system in action.

Anthony Watts [KA9NWM](https://www.qrz.com/db/KA9NWM){:target="_blank"} has been a licensed amateur radio operator since 1982. By trade he's a broadcast meteorologist and systems engineer, and he brings both of those backgrounds directly into Helioclock, a real-time Earth clock and situational awareness kiosk built for the ham radio community. Anthony builds, ships, and supports Helioclock entirely on his own, from software development to manufacturing and customer support. The project began as a spiritual successor to HamClock after its original developer became a Silent Key in early 2026.
