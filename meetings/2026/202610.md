## Details

Kent Olsen, [N6WT](https://www.qrz.com/db/N6WT){:target="_blank"}, began his ham radio journey in 1998, when a fellow member of his off-road club, the **Desert Dawgs**, encouraged the group to earn their Technician licenses. Now retired after 26 years as a UPS feeder driver, Kent lives on 16 acres outside Arroyo Grande with his wife, Gail [KJ6CPH](https://www.qrz.com/db/KJ6CPH){:target="_blank"}.

He earned his General and Extra licenses in 2014, and today he chases DX (318 DXCC confirmed, DXCC Challenge 2265), enjoys contesting, and operates **Summits on the Air** - a great way to stay in shape and get out of the shack.
